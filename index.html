关于我

2024年8月起任华东师范大学数学科学学院青年研究员。 2021–2024年于中科院数学与系统科学研究院晨兴数学中心从事博士后研究（合作导师：付保华）。 2020–2021年曾任巴黎萨克雷大学 ATER。 博士毕业于巴黎萨克雷大学（2017–2021，导师：Joël Merker, Việt-Anh Nguyễn）。

About

I am a Young Research Fellow at the School of Mathematical Sciences, East China Normal University, since August 2024. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Morningside Center of Mathematics, Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2021–2024, mentored by Baohua Fu). I was an ATER at Université Paris-Saclay (2020–2021). I received my Ph.D. from Université Paris-Saclay (2017–2021, supervised by Joël Merker and Việt-Anh Nguyễn).

Research Interests

  • Complex dynamics: Holomorphic foliations and current theory
  • Several complex variables: Oka manifold theory and Nevanlinna theory
