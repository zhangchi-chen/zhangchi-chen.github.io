1. 1. Which Mₙ proves that Σ sin(nx)/(n√n) converges uniformly on ℝ?
  1. A1/n
  2. B1/(n√n)
  3. C1/√n
    4. Correct: B. |sin(nx)| ≤ 1 gives |uₙ(x)| ≤ 1/(n√n), and Σ1/n^(3/2) converges (p = 3/2 > 1); A and C are upper bounds whose series diverge.
2. 2. On [−1/2, 1/2], which fence works for Σ n xⁿ?
  1. AMₙ = n/2ⁿ
  2. BMₙ = 1/2ⁿ
  3. CMₙ = n
    4. Correct: A. |n xⁿ| ≤ n/2ⁿ there, and Σ n/2ⁿ converges by the ratio test (ratio → 1/2); B is not an upper bound for n ≥ 2 (take x = 1/2), and ΣC diverges.
3. 3. In the two-line proof of the M-test, what does the triangle inequality do?
  1. AIt shows that ΣMₙ converges.
  2. BAt every x it bounds |uₙ₊₁(x) + ⋯ + uₙ₊ₚ(x)| by Mₙ₊₁ + ⋯ + Mₙ₊ₚ.
  3. CIt computes the sum function.
    4. Correct: B. line one takes N from the Cauchy criterion for ΣMₙ; line two uses the triangle inequality and |uₖ(x)| ≤ Mₖ to hand that N to every x, so Theorem 13.3 applies.
4. 4. Classify, in order: Σxⁿ on [−1/2, 1/2]; Σxⁿ on (−1, 1); Σ sin(nx)/n² on ℝ.
  1. Auniform; not uniform; uniform
  2. Buniform; uniform; not uniform
  3. Cnot uniform; not uniform; uniform
    4. Correct: A. fence (1/2)ⁿ on [−1/2, 1/2]; on (−1, 1) the terms fail the corollary (sup |xⁿ| = 1); fence 1/n² on ℝ.
5. 5. The fastest correct refutation of uniform convergence of Σxⁿ on (0, 1)?
  1. AFor every n, sup over (0, 1) of xⁿ is 1, so the terms do not tend to 0 uniformly.
  2. BThe series diverges at x = 1/2.
  3. CEvery term xⁿ is continuous on (0, 1).
    4. Correct: A. that is the corollary's check failing; B is false (the series converges at 1/2, to 2), and C is true but proves nothing.
6. 6. True or false: Σxⁿ converges uniformly on (−1, 1) because it converges uniformly on every [−a, a] with 0 < a < 1.
  1. ATrue: (−1, 1) is the union of the [−a, a].
  2. BFalse: uniformity on every closed subinterval does not upgrade to the open interval.
  3. CTrue: the M-test with Mₙ = aⁿ covers (−1, 1).
    4. Correct: B. the fence totals 1/(1 − a) blow up as a → 1, and sup over (−1, 1) of |xⁿ| is 1, so the series is uniform on closed subintervals only.
