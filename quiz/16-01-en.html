Plane point sets: does the boundary count as one of us

← Notes · Index
1. 1. For D = {1 ≤ x² + y² < 4}, the point (2, 0) is:
  1. Aan interior point of D, in D
  2. Ba boundary point and an accumulation point of D, not in D
  3. Can isolated point of D
    4. Correct: B. 2² + 0² = 4 fails < 4, yet every disk around (2, 0) contains points (2 − 1/n, 0) of D and the point (2, 0) outside D.
2. 2. For E = {xy > 0}, the origin O is:
  1. Aa boundary point and an accumulation point of E, not in E
  2. Ban exterior point of E
  3. Can interior point of E
    4. Correct: A. 0 · 0 = 0 is not > 0; every disk around O contains (1/n, 1/n) ∈ E and points of the axes, which are not in E.
3. 3. The set {x² + y² ≤ 1} is:
  1. Aopen, and an open region
  2. Bneither open nor closed
  3. Cclosed, and a closed region
    4. Correct: C. it is the open region {x² + y² < 1} together with its boundary, the unit circle; (1, 0) is in it but not interior, so it is not open.
4. 4. Compare {xy > 0} and [0, 1] × [0, 1]:
  1. Aboth are regions
  2. B{xy > 0} is open but not a region; [0, 1] × [0, 1] is a closed region
  3. C{xy > 0} is closed; [0, 1] × [0, 1] is open
    4. Correct: B. every polygonal line between the two quadrants meets an axis, so {xy > 0} is not connected; the square is the open square plus its four sides.
5. 5. If A is an accumulation point of E, then:
  1. Aevery neighborhood of A contains infinitely many points of E
  2. BA must belong to E
  3. Csome neighborhood of A contains exactly one point of E
    4. Correct: A. with only finitely many points, a small enough punctured disk would hold none; B fails for O and S = {(1/n, 0)}.
6. 6. “A set that is not open is closed.”
  1. Atrue
  2. Btrue for bounded sets
  3. Cfalse: 1 ≤ x² + y² < 4 is neither
    4. Correct: C. the bounded ring keeps its inner circle (not open) and misses its outer circle (not closed).
